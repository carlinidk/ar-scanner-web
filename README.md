## 🌐 Project Overview

**AR Scanner Web** is a browser-based augmented reality experience that lets users:
- 📱 Scan AR markers or QR codes  
- 👕 View interactive 3D fashion models directly in your browser  
- 🧠 Blend digital creativity with real-world fashion experiences

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **Open the web app** on your mobile or desktop browser  
2. **Allow camera access** when prompted  
3. **Scan an AR marker / QR pattern**  
4. Watch your **3D fashion model** come to life in real space!
